Features

What DriverSaver does

🚗

All your vehicles

Add every car, truck, bike or machine, each with its own photo, odometer and history.

⛽

Fuel and economy

Log fill-ups in seconds and DriverSaver works out your fuel economy over time.

🔧

Service records

Keep a complete service and maintenance history, ready to show a mechanic or a buyer.

🛞

Tyres and expenses

Track tyre sets, insurance, registration, repairs and any other running cost.

🔔

Maintenance reminders

Get reminded when a service is due, by date, by odometer, or both.

🧾

Scan receipts

Scan a fuel receipt and DriverSaver fills in the date, litres, price and station for you.

📊

Reports and exports

See charts of where your money goes and export a PDF vehicle report or CSV file.

⌚

Apple Watch

Log a fuel fill-up right from your wrist at the pump.

☁️

iCloud sync and backups

Your records sync across your devices through your private iCloud, with JSON backups and CSV import.

Your privacy
No account, no ads, no analytics and no location tracking. Your data stays on your devices and in your own private iCloud.
