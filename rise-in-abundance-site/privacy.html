Privacy Policy

Effective date: 3 November 2026

This policy explains how Rise in Abundance (the "App"), made by Glenn Bonnet ("I", "me"), handles your information. The short version: your affirmations and voice recordings stay on your device, there is no account, and I never sell your information.

1. What you create in the App

The App lets you create affirmations, voice recordings, programs, alarms, goals, milestones, intentions and settings, and add your own sound files and images. This content is stored on your device. I do not have access to it, and it is not uploaded to me or to anyone else.

If your device backs up to iCloud, this content may be included in that backup under Apple's privacy policy.

2. Microphone, notifications and files

  • Microphone: used only when you choose to record an affirmation. Recordings are saved on your device.
  • Alarms and notifications: used only to wake you with the alarms you set and to deliver the intention reminders you turn on.
  • Files and photos: sound files and images you add are copied into the App on your device.

You can change these permissions at any time in your device Settings.

3. Information collected automatically

The App does not include advertising or third-party tracking. Apple may share anonymous crash reports and usage statistics with me if you have agreed to share them with app developers in your device settings. These do not identify you personally.

4. Purchases

Optional premium sounds are sold as in-app purchases, which are handled entirely by Apple. I never receive or store your payment details.

5. Sharing

I do not sell, rent or trade your information. Nothing you create in the App is shared with me or with third parties.

6. When you contact me

If you email me, I receive your email address and whatever you include in your message. I use it only to reply to you, and I will delete the correspondence if you ask.

7. This website

This website does not use cookies or tracking. The hosting provider may keep standard server logs, such as IP addresses, for security. The site loads fonts from Google Fonts.

8. Deleting your data

You can delete any affirmation, recording, program or alarm inside the App. Deleting the App removes all of its data from your device.

9. Children

The App is not directed at children under 13, and I do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

10. Your rights and changes to this policy

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal information held about you. Because the App doesn't send your content to me, in most cases I hold none. I may update this policy from time to time and will post changes here with a new effective date.

11. Contact

Questions about this policy? Email info@glennbonnet.com.
