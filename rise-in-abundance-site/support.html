Alarms

My alarm didn't go off. What should I check?

Make sure the alarm is switched on in the app and set for today's day of the week. Then check that Alarms are allowed for Rise in Abundance in Settings → Apps → Rise in Abundance. If you declined the alarm permission when the app first asked, turn it on there.

Can I have alarms on some days only?

Yes. When you create or edit an alarm, choose the days it repeats, for example Monday to Friday.

How do I change which program an alarm plays?

Tap Edit Alarm on the Home screen and choose a different program.

Recording affirmations

The app can't hear me when I record.

Rise in Abundance needs microphone access to record your voice. Go to Settings → Privacy & Security → Microphone and turn it on for Rise in Abundance.

How do I re-record an affirmation?

Open the affirmation in My Voice and record again. The new recording replaces the old one.

If I delete a program, do I lose my recordings?

No. Your recorded affirmations are kept. Alarms that used the deleted program switch to another program.

Programs and sounds

How do I create my own program?

Go to Programs, tap + and choose your affirmations, sounds, length and volume. Save your changes so the program plays exactly what you see.

Can I use my own sounds?

Yes. In the program editor, tap + to add a sound file from your device.

Premium sounds

What are premium sounds?

Premium sounds are optional extra soundscapes you can buy in the app to use in your programs. Everything else works without them.

How do I restore a purchase?

Sign in with the same Apple ID you used to buy, open the premium sounds store in Programs and tap Restore.
