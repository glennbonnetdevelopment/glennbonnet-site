HomeSaver

Your home, accounted for.

iPhone

HomeSaver is a home inventory and upkeep app. Record what you own, where it is and what it cost, keep receipts and serial numbers safe, and stay on top of home maintenance, for one home or several.

Features

What HomeSaver does

🏠

Every home

Track a main house, cabin, rental or condo, each with its own rooms and items.

📦

Detailed inventory

Record category, location, quantity, cost, purchase date, brand, model, serial number and notes.

🧾

Photos and receipts

Attach item photos and receipt photos or PDFs, ready for a warranty or insurance claim.

🔍

Search and scan

Find anything in seconds by name, brand or serial number, or scan a barcode.

🔧

Maintenance reminders

Add standard home tasks like furnace filters, or your own, and get reminded when they are due.

🛡️

Insurance and trades

Keep your insurance policies and trusted tradespeople with the homes they cover.

🚚

Moving and storage

Pack items into boxes and see what is packed when you move.

📊

Reports and exports

Totals by category, location or year with charts, printable PDF reports and CSV import and export.

Your privacy
No account and no tracking. Your inventory is stored on your device and in your own iCloud, and I don't have access to it.
