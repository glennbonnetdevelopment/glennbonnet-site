PlantMaxer Privacy Policy

Effective date: 10 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Glenn Bonnet ("I", "me", "my") handles information when you use PlantMaxer (the "App"). It applies only to PlantMaxer. My other apps each have their own policy, listed on the privacy page.

Your privacy matters. I collect as little information as possible, and I never sell your personal information.

1. Information you enter in the App

PlantMaxer lets you record information for your own use, such as plants, feeds and nutrient readings, nutrient schedules, harvests, varieties, seed inventory, equipment, climate readings, photos and settings.

This information is stored on your device and, if you are signed in to iCloud, synced through your own private iCloud database, which only you can access. I do not have access to the content you enter. Backups, spreadsheets, labels and reports are created only when you ask, and go wherever you choose to save or share them.

2. Permissions

  • Camera and photos: used only when you choose to take or add plant photos, or to scan PlantMaxer barcode labels.
  • Bluetooth: if you use a supported Bluetooth temperature and humidity sensor, PlantMaxer reads it while the app is open and saves the readings with your own data.

PlantMaxer does not use your location and does not send you notifications. You can change any permission at any time in your device settings.

3. Information collected automatically

PlantMaxer does not collect any analytics, usage or tracking data.

4. Purchases and subscriptions

If you make an in-app purchase or subscribe, payment is handled entirely by the Apple App Store. I never receive or store your payment card details.

5. How information is used

Any information I do receive is used only to:

  • provide, maintain and improve the App;
  • fix bugs and resolve technical problems;
  • reply to you when you contact me for support.

6. Sharing of information

I do not sell, rent or trade your personal information. Information is only shared with service providers needed to run the App (such as the App Store), or where required by law.

7. When you contact me

If you email me, I will receive your email address and anything you include in your message. I use this only to respond to you, and I do not add you to any mailing list without your permission.

8. Data retention and deletion

Because your data is stored on your device, you can delete it at any time within the App or by uninstalling the App. Data stored in your own iCloud can be managed through your device or iCloud settings. If you have emailed me and would like that correspondence deleted, just ask.

9. Children's privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13, and I do not knowingly collect personal information from children. PlantMaxer is rated 18+.

10. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct or delete personal information held about you, or to object to how it is used. To make a request, contact me using the details below.

11. Changes to this policy

I may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

12. Contact

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please email info@glennbonnet.com.
