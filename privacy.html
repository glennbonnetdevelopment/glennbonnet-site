Privacy Policy

Effective date: 3 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Glenn Bonnet ("I", "me", "my") handles information when you use my apps — DriverSaver, Rise In Abundance, HomeSaver, PlantMaxer and AgSaver (together, the "Apps") — and this website, glennbonnet.com.

Your privacy matters. I collect as little information as possible, and I never sell your personal information.

1. Information you enter in the Apps

The Apps let you record information for your own use, such as:

  • DriverSaver: vehicle and machinery details, expenses, fuel purchases, odometer or hour readings, and notes.
  • HomeSaver: descriptions, photos, values, locations and notes about your personal belongings.
  • Rise In Abundance: affirmations, intentions, favourites and journal-style entries you create.
  • PlantMaxer: plants, feeds and nutrient readings, nutrient schedules, harvests, seed inventory, equipment, photos and settings.
  • AgSaver: agricultural equipment details, service records, expenses, work sessions and hour logs, fuel, photos and notes.

This information is stored on your device. If you turn on a backup or sync feature (for example, iCloud or Google backup), it is also stored with that provider under their privacy policy. I do not have access to the content you enter.

AgSaver and PlantMaxer with iCloud: if you are signed in to iCloud, these Apps sync your data through your own private iCloud database, which only you can access. Backups, spreadsheets, labels and reports are created only when you ask, and go wherever you choose to save or share them.

2. Photos, camera and other permissions

Some features may ask for access to your camera, photo library or notifications (for example, to add a photo of an item or a receipt, to take plant photos or scan PlantMaxer barcode labels, or to send daily affirmation reminders). This access is only used for the feature you choose, and you can change it at any time in your device settings.

Location (AgSaver): AgSaver uses your location only while a work session is running, to record the path your machine travels so you can see where you worked on a map. The route is saved with your own data and is not sent to me.

3. Information collected automatically

The Apps may collect limited, non-identifying technical information, such as crash reports and basic usage statistics, to help fix bugs and improve the Apps. This information is not used to identify you personally. PlantMaxer does not collect any analytics, usage or tracking data.

4. Purchases and subscriptions

If you make an in-app purchase or subscribe, payment is handled entirely by the Apple App Store or Google Play. I never receive or store your payment card details.

5. How information is used

Any information I do receive is used only to:

  • provide, maintain and improve the Apps;
  • fix bugs and resolve technical problems;
  • reply to you when you contact me for support.

6. Sharing of information

I do not sell, rent or trade your personal information. Information is only shared with service providers needed to run the Apps (such as the app stores and crash-reporting tools), or where required by law.

7. When you contact me

If you email me, I will receive your email address and anything you include in your message. I use this only to respond to you, and I do not add you to any mailing list without your permission.

8. This website

glennbonnet.com is a simple informational website hosted on GitHub Pages. It does not use cookies or tracking. The hosting provider may keep standard server logs (such as IP addresses) for security purposes. The site loads fonts from Google Fonts.

9. Data retention and deletion

Because your data is stored on your device, you can delete it at any time by deleting it within the App or uninstalling the App. Data stored in your own cloud backup can be managed through your device or cloud account settings. If you have emailed me and would like that correspondence deleted, just ask.

10. Children's privacy

The Apps are not directed at children under 13, and I do not knowingly collect personal information from children. PlantMaxer is rated 18+.

11. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct or delete personal information held about you, or to object to how it is used. To make a request, contact me using the details below.

12. Changes to this policy

I may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

13. Contact

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please email info@glennbonnet.com.
