DriverSaver

Track expenses and fuel for your vehicles and machinery.

Can I track more than one vehicle or machine?

Yes. Add each vehicle or piece of machinery separately, and its expenses and fuel records are kept apart so you can see what each one costs to run.

How do I correct a wrong entry?

Open the expense or fuel entry you want to change, edit the details, and save. You can also delete an entry you no longer need.

Rise In Abundance

An affirmation and manifestation universe.

How do I get daily affirmation reminders?

Turn on reminders in the app and allow notifications when asked. If you don't receive them, check that notifications are enabled for Rise In Abundance in your device settings.

Can I write my own affirmations?

Yes. Create your own affirmations and intentions and keep them alongside your favourites.

HomeSaver

Keep a record of your personal belongings.

Why should I record my belongings?

A record of what you own — with photos, values and where items are kept — is useful for insurance claims, moving house, or simply knowing what you have.

Can I add photos of my items?

Yes. Allow camera or photo library access when asked. If you declined earlier, you can turn access back on in your device settings.

PlantMaxer

A grow journal for your plants, feeds, harvests and seeds.

How do I sync PlantMaxer between my iPhone and iPad?

Sign in to the same Apple ID on both devices and make sure iCloud is turned on for PlantMaxer in Settings → your name → iCloud. Your plants, feeds and seeds sync through your own private iCloud storage.

How do I back up or move my data?

Go to Organize → Import & Export to create a backup file or export a spreadsheet. Save it to Files, iCloud Drive or anywhere you like, and restore it from the same screen.

How do I print and scan labels?

Plants, seed packets and seed boxes each get a barcode. Print labels from Organize → Labels using any AirPrint printer (or share them as a PDF), then use the scan button to jump straight to an item. Allow camera access when asked.

What happens when I harvest a plant?

Harvested plants move out of your active list into the harvest archive, so their feeding history is kept for future reference.

AgSaver

Track service, expenses and work for your agricultural equipment.

Can I track more than one machine?

Yes. Add each tractor, implement or piece of equipment separately, and its service history, expenses and work records are kept apart so you can see what each one costs to run.

How do I keep on top of servicing?

Record each service with the date and hour reading, and AgSaver keeps a full service history for every machine. Set reminders so you know when the next service is due, and mark them done when the work is finished.

Why does AgSaver ask for my location?

Location is only used while a work session is running, to record the path your machine travels so you can see where you worked on a map. If you declined earlier, you can turn it back on in Settings → Privacy & Security → Location Services → AgSaver.

How do I sync AgSaver between devices?

Sign in to the same Apple ID on each device with iCloud turned on. Your equipment and records sync through your own private iCloud storage, and the Apple Watch app works alongside your iPhone.

General questions

Will I lose my data if I get a new phone?

Your data is stored on your device. Make sure your device backups (iCloud or Google) are turned on before switching phones so your data can be restored.

How do I restore a purchase?

Sign in with the same Apple ID or Google account you used to buy, then use the "Restore Purchases" option in the app's settings.

How do I cancel a subscription?

Subscriptions are managed by your app store. On iPhone, go to Settings → your name → Subscriptions. On Android, open Google Play → Profile → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions.
