DriverSaver

Track expenses and fuel for your vehicles and machinery.

Can I track more than one vehicle or machine?

Yes. Add each vehicle or piece of machinery separately, and its expenses and fuel records are kept apart so you can see what each one costs to run.

How do I correct a wrong entry?

Open the expense or fuel entry you want to change, edit the details, and save. You can also delete an entry you no longer need.

Rise In Abundance

An affirmation and manifestation universe.

How do I get daily affirmation reminders?

Turn on reminders in the app and allow notifications when asked. If you don't receive them, check that notifications are enabled for Rise In Abundance in your device settings.

Can I write my own affirmations?

Yes. Create your own affirmations and intentions and keep them alongside your favourites.

HomeSaver

Keep a record of your personal belongings.

Why should I record my belongings?

A record of what you own — with photos, values and where items are kept — is useful for insurance claims, moving house, or simply knowing what you have.

Can I add photos of my items?

Yes. Allow camera or photo library access when asked. If you declined earlier, you can turn access back on in your device settings.

General questions

Will I lose my data if I get a new phone?

Your data is stored on your device. Make sure your device backups (iCloud or Google) are turned on before switching phones so your data can be restored.

How do I restore a purchase?

Sign in with the same Apple ID or Google account you used to buy, then use the "Restore Purchases" option in the app's settings.

How do I cancel a subscription?

Subscriptions are managed by your app store. On iPhone, go to Settings → your name → Subscriptions. On Android, open Google Play → Profile → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions.
