Getting Started

The four tabs

Work is for the day's jobs, the map and your fields. Herd lists every animal by group. Events shows everything recorded. More has reports, settings, reminders, inventory, exports and this help.

First steps

  1. Add your fields (Work → New Field) and map them.
  2. Add groups (Herd → + → New Group), e.g. Cow Herd, Bulls, Replacement Heifers, and give each a field.
  3. Add animals (Herd → + → Add Animal).
  4. Record what you do as you do it: moves, vaccinations, feeding, sales.

Try it with sample data

Load Sample Data (on an empty ranch or in More) creates a separate “Sample Ranch” with fields, animals and records to explore. Delete it from More → Ranches when you're done.

Works without signal

Everything saves on your phone first. If you're signed in to iCloud it syncs when you have signal.

Ranches & Crew

Switching ranch

Tap the ranch name at the top of the Work page to switch, or go to More → Ranches. Each ranch is completely separate: its own animals, fields, records, reports, Premises ID and currency.

Adding a ranch

More → Ranches → New Ranch. Swipe a ranch you own to rename or delete it. Deleting removes it from every device and from everyone it's shared with.

Inviting crew

Open the ranch, then More → Crew → Invite Crew. Send the link by Messages, Mail or AirDrop. Choose “Can make changes” or “View only” for each person. Use Manage to change access or remove someone, or Stop Sharing to remove everyone.

Joining someone's ranch

Tap the invitation they send you. Their ranch is added next to yours; nothing of yours is changed. It shows “Shared by” their name.

Leaving

Swipe the ranch in More → Ranches and tap Leave, or More → Crew → Leave Ranch. If the owner removes you or stops sharing, the ranch is removed from your phone automatically.

View only

On a view-only ranch you can see everything but the editing buttons are turned off.

Fields & the Map

Adding a field

Work → New Field. Enter the name and acres, plus water and fence notes if you like.

Mapping a boundary

Open the field → Map Boundary. Tap each corner in order on the satellite map. Once it's closed, tap near an edge to add a corner there. Use Undo or Clear to fix mistakes. The area is shown as you go.

Reading the map

Red outline: a breeding bull is in with females. Yellow: stocked. White: empty. Each field's label shows head, females and males, groups, Bull in, and when it was last fed. Tap a field to open it.

Field page

Shows the animals, groups, grazing days and AUMs, feeding, move history, and the bull in / bull out log with the earliest expected calving date.

Printable map

More → Reports → Ranch Map makes a printable PDF with all fields, their head counts and groups, and a table of every field.

Herd, Groups & Animals

Herd page

Animals are listed by group, then Ungrouped. Use Show to filter (Active, Females, Bulls, Steers, Sold/Dead, All), Filter by date for a date range, and search by tag, RFID, name or tag colour.

Selecting several

Tap Select, tick animals (or Select All in a group), then put them in a group, move them, or record an event for all of them.

Groups

Each group's ⋯ menu: details, move the whole group, record an event, sell the group, rename, delete. Putting animals in a group that has a field also moves them there.

An animal's page

Photo, tag and RFID, details, weights and daily gain, breeding record, money (purchase, feed, drug and shipping costs, sale, profit), feeding, parents and family tree, and full history.

Tags

Use Change Tag on the animal's page so the old tag stays in the history. RFIDs are 15 digits; Canadian CCIA tags start with 124.

Photos

Tap the camera button on an animal's page to take or choose a photo.

Parents & family tree

Set the sire and dam on the Edit page (or the sire when recording a calf). Family Tree shows grandparents, parents and calves.

Moving & Bull In / Out

Moving animals

Work → Move Animals, pick the animals or whole groups, choose the field. Or use Move on an animal's, group's or field's page.

Bull in / bull out

A breeding bull is a male that isn't castrated and is marked Breeding bull. When a move puts a bull with females, Bull In is recorded for that field; when they're separated, Bull Out. The Move screen shows what will be recorded, and you can turn it off. Bull calves recorded with Record Calf don't count.

Inbreeding warning

If a move would put a bull in with his daughter, granddaughter, dam, sister or other close relative, the Move screen and the field's page show a Related animals warning.

Roundups & Counting In

Starting a roundup

Tap Round Up on the Work page (or More → Round Ups → +). Pick the field to gather, optionally just one group in it, and the field they're going to. Add the transportation cost and trucker if there was one. Choose All now, count later (everyone is moved straight away) or As they're counted (each animal moves when you count it). Bull in / out and the related-animals warning work as for any move.

Counting in later

Open the roundup from Count pending on the Work page or More → Round Ups, any time later and in as many sittings as you like, on any phone that has the ranch. Scan tags with an EID wand, Bluetooth reader or the camera, type them, or tap animals in the Not seen list. It shows how many of the expected animals are counted. A tag from another field or group can be added as an extra; it's moved here.

Finishing the count

Tap Finish Count. For each animal not seen choose Left behind (put back in the field it came from, dated the roundup day, and taken off the trucking bill), Missing (stays where the records say and is flagged – Herd → Show → Missing), Was there, or Dead. The transportation cost is re-split over the animals that actually made the trip.

Reminder

An open roundup appears in Reminders as Count pending two days after the move.

When a missing animal turns up

A missing animal's page shows Missing since … with a Found It button: say where it was – with the herd (nothing changes), back in the field it came from (it never made the trip: put back, its trucking share removed and re-split), somewhere else (moved there, optional transport cost) or found dead. It's also marked found automatically when it's scanned in Sort or a round up count, moved, weighed, treated or sold, with a Found note in its history. The closed round up then shows it as Found later, also in its summary PDF.

Missing report

More → Reports → Missing lists every missing animal, how many days it's been gone and which round up, with Found It. After 30 days you can Write Off as Lost, which takes it off the herd count as a death loss.

Transport Costs

Trucking on a move

Every Move screen has an optional Transportation cost and trucker. The cost is split evenly over the animals moved, shown on each animal's page as Trucking / transport, and taken off its profit. The year-end summary lists it as Trucking & transport (moves).

Events & Health

Recording an event

Work → Record Event (or from an animal or group): branding, medication, vaccination, weighing, preg check, castrated, weaned, bull in/out, sold, note.

Withdrawal periods

Enter the withdrawal days from the drug label. The animal shows “Safe to ship after” that date in orange until it passes. The Events tab and More → Health & Withdrawals list animals not safe to ship yet, and selling one asks you to confirm.

Boosters

On a vaccination, enter “Booster in __ days” and a reminder is made for the booster.

Drugs from inventory

Pick the product from inventory and the dose per head: stock is counted down and each animal's drug cost is recorded.

Feeding & Inventory

Recording feed

Work → Feed (or Feed This Field). Choose the field or group, feed, amount and cost. The amount and cost are split evenly over the animals fed and added to each animal's feed cost.

Inventory

More → Inventory: hay, grain, mineral, vaccines and drugs with quantity, cost per unit and a low-stock warning. Restock adds to the stock and keeps an average cost. Feeding and treatments picked “From inventory” count it down.

Contacts

More → Contacts: your vet, suppliers, buyers and truckers. Tap a number to call.

Calving, Breeding & Culling

Recording a calf

Open the cow → Record Calf: tag, sex, birth weight, calving ease (1 no help … 5 abnormal), calf vigour (1 up and nursing … 5 dead at birth) and the sire.

Preg checks

Record Event → Preg Check: Pregnant (with estimated days bred, which gives the expected calving date) or Open.

Cull list

More → Reports → Cull List shows animals open at preg check, 10 years or older, with a hard calving or a weak or lost calf, or marked to cull on their Edit page. Swipe to mark or unmark.

Calving & weaning report

Calves born, heifers and bulls, average birth weight, calving ease scores, expected calving from bull-in dates, and weaning weights.

Weighing

Record Event → Weighing on an animal. Its page shows the daily gain between the last two weigh-ins and over all its weights.

Weaning weight

Record Event → Weaned with the weight. The calf's page shows its weaning weight and that weight as a percentage of its dam's weight.

Selling & Shipping Reports

Selling one animal

Animal's page → Mark Sold or Dead, or Record Event → Sold. Enter the sale weight and price, and the buyer, trucker and shipping cost.

Selling a group

A group's ⋯ menu → Sell Group, or select animals → Record Event → Sold. Enter the total proceeds, total weight and shipping cost; they're divided evenly per head. You can leave out animals still in a withdrawal period.

Selling a load

Tap Sell Cattle on the Work page (beside Buy Cattle). Step 1: date, auction mart or buyer, destination Premises ID, trucker, gross proceeds, shipping, commission & fees, weight, manifest and brand inspection numbers. Step 2: pick the animals by whole group, by ticking them, or by scanning RFIDs or typing tags (EID wand, Bluetooth reader or camera). Animals not safe to ship yet are flagged. Step 3: review each animal's sale price, costs, net and profit, weigh each animal and split by weight if you like, then tap Sell. A shipping report is saved and opens.

Shipping reports

Every sale saves a printable shipping report and opens it. Find them all in More → Historical Shipping Reports. Use Print or Share at the top.

Profit

Each animal's profit is its sale price less shipping, purchase price, feed and drug costs. More → Reports → Sales & Profit adds it all up for a period.

Buying Cattle

Entering a load

Tap Buy Cattle on the Work page (or Herd → + → Buy a Load of Cattle, or More → Purchases → +). Step 1 is the load: date, auction mart or private sale, seller, the seller's Premises ID, head on the load, price paid, trucking, commission and fees, total weight, manifest and brand inspection numbers.

Adding the animals

Step 2: set what's the same for all of them (kind, breed, approximate age, arrival weight or weigh each one). Then for each animal scan its RFID with an EID wand, Bluetooth reader or the camera, or type it, and check the visual tag number and colour. Tag numbers count up by themselves. The count shows how many of the load you've entered. Duplicate RFIDs or tags are caught before they're added.

Arrival

Step 3: choose a group (or make a new one) and the receiving field. The animals are moved there, so bull in / out is worked out. You can also give every animal a vaccination or treatment on arrival, drawn from inventory.

How the cost is split

Price + trucking + fees are divided evenly per head, or by arrival weight if you weighed each animal and turn on Split by weight. Each animal's share becomes its purchase price, so profit, Sales & Profit and the year-end summary include freight and fees.

Purchase records

More → Purchases lists every load with its total cost, cost per head and per lb, the animals, and a printable Purchase Report PDF. Deleting a purchase record keeps the animals.

CLTS

Bought animals appear in the CLTS export as move-ins on the load's date with the seller and the seller's Premises ID.

Sorting & EID Readers

Sorting at the chute

Work → Sort. Scan a tag with the camera, type it, or use an EID reader. Then tap the field the animal goes to. It's moved right away and the screen is ready for the next one.

Bluetooth wands

Wands that work as a Bluetooth keyboard are paired in the iPhone's Settings → Bluetooth; choose EID Reader in Sort and scan. Bluetooth LE readers connect in More → EID Reader and reconnect by themselves.

Same tag number

If two animals share a number, you're asked which one.

Pasture & Grazing

Grazing days and AUMs

Each field shows days grazed and rested, how long it's been grazed or rested, AUMs used and the stocking rate, worked out from move history. More → Reports → Pasture & Grazing shows every field.

Animal units

Set how many animal units each kind counts as in More → Animal Units (a 1,000 lb cow is 1 AU). Set calves to 0 if you count pairs.

What's reminded

More → Reminders lists boosters, bull out at the end of the breeding season, preg check after bull out, and the day each animal is safe to ship, plus reminders you add with +.

Notifications

Reports, Exports & CLTS

Reports

More → Reports: Ranch Map, Herd Inventory, Sales & Profit, Feed & Feed Cost, Calving & Weaning, Pasture & Grazing, Cull List, Health & Withdrawals. Most have a period picker.

Excel and CSV

Currency

Troubleshooting

Not syncing

Check More shows your iCloud status. You must be signed in to iCloud in the Settings app, and the app needs signal to send changes. Changes made offline are kept and sent later.

Crew can't see the ranch

They need RanchMaster installed and must tap the invitation on the phone signed in to the Apple Account you invited. Check More → Crew shows them as accepted.

Map is blank

The satellite map and the Ranch Map PDF need an internet connection to draw. Field outlines and everything else work offline.

Camera won't read tags

Hold the tag flat and well lit, fill most of the frame, and tap a highlighted number if it isn't picked up. Camera scanning needs a newer iPhone; use Keyboard or an EID wand otherwise.

A number I typed was lost

Numbers are saved as you type. If something still looks wrong, open the record again and check it.

Can't edit anything

The ranch was shared with you as View only. Ask the owner to change your access in their More → Crew.

Deleted something by mistake

Deleted records can't be brought back in the app, so swipe carefully. Sold and dead animals are kept; use Mark Sold or Dead instead of deleting.

Backup & Restore

What's backed up

Your own ranches: every animal, field and map, group, event, feeding, sale, purchase, round up, inventory item, contact, reminder and photo, plus the ranch's Premises ID and currency and your animal-unit settings. Ranches shared with you aren't included – their owner backs them up.

Backing up

More → Backup & Restore → Back Up Now (all your ranches, or one). Then Save or Send This Backup to put a copy in iCloud Drive, on a computer, or in an email, so it's safe if the phone is lost.

Automatic backups

With Back up automatically each day on, a backup is made when you leave the app, at most once a day. The last 10 are kept in Files → On My iPhone → RanchMaster → Backups.

Restoring

Tap a backup in the list, choose Restore from a File, or open a .ranchbackup file from Files, Mail or AirDrop. You see what's in it first. Restore as a New Ranch adds a copy beside your ranches without changing anything. Replace puts the ranch back exactly as it was in the backup; you type the ranch name to confirm, and a backup of it as it is now is made first. If the ranch is shared, the change reaches your crew too.

Damaged files

Every backup has a checksum. A damaged or edited file is refused and nothing is changed.

iCloud & Backup

Sync

When you're signed in to iCloud, every ranch syncs to your other iPhones and iPads and to your crew, and is backed up in iCloud. The status line at the top of More shows when it last synced.

No signal

Keep working. Changes are saved on the phone and sent when you're back in range.

Two people, same record

If two people change the same animal at the same time, the change sent last is kept.
