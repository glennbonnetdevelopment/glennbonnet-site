Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 12, 2026

RanchMaster is designed so your ranch records belong to you. We do not sell, rent, or share your personal or operational data with third parties, advertisers, or data brokers.

1. Data Storage & iCloud Sync

All herd, pasture, feeding, inventory, contact, and event data you enter in RanchMaster is stored locally on your device using Apple’s SwiftData framework and, when enabled, synced across your devices and invited crew members through your private Apple iCloud (CloudKit) account. We do not operate separate servers that store your ranch database.

2. Camera, Photos & Bluetooth

  • Camera & Photo Library: Used only when you choose to take or attach animal photos or scan ear tags in Sort mode.
  • Bluetooth: Used only when you choose to connect an EID/RFID reader.
  • Notifications: Used only when you enable reminders (e.g., vaccine boosters, bull-out dates, preg checks, or withdrawal clearances).

3. Crew Sharing

When you invite crew members to a ranch, Apple’s CloudKit sharing shares only that specific ranch’s zone with the people you explicitly invite. You can change permissions or revoke access at any time in More → Crew.

4. Analytics & Tracking

RanchMaster does not use third-party advertising SDKs or cross-app tracking.

5. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact info@glennbonnet.com.
